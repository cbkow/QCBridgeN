QCBridgeN sends what QCView shows and plays to the network as an NDI® source.

This installs QCBridgeN.app in /Applications and registers it with QCView
(/Library/Application Support/QCView/Outputs/ndi.json). Afterwards, in
QCView, open Settings ▸ Output and switch "QCBridgeN (NDI)" on: QCView
starts QCBridgeN, which appears in the menu bar, and the source "QCView"
appears to NDI receivers on the network. Nothing is sent until the switch
is on.

The NDI runtime is included in the application. QCView 2.5.4 or later is
required.
